The brief

The client runs accredited workplace courses, paediatric training for childcare professionals and relaxed community classes for families. Each audience buys differently: individuals want to pick a date and pay, while organisations want a price, an invoice and control over who attends.

She wanted a public site that stays simple and warm, and an admin system powerful enough that she never has to touch code, chase payments by hand or keep bookings in a spreadsheet.

Course catalogue and calendar One source of truth for every session

Open classes

Parents, grandparents, carers, community groups

  1. Pick a course and date from live availability
  2. Choose seats and add each attendee's name
  3. Pay in full through secure checkout
  4. Confirmation email arrives instantly
  5. Reminder sent 48 hours before the class

Seats are held while the customer pays, then released automatically if payment fails, so a class can never be overbooked.

Workplace training

Employers, nurseries, childminders, sports clubs

  1. Send an enquiry with headcount, location and dates
  2. Receive a tailored quote built from a template
  3. Secure the date by paying a deposit online
  4. Add attendee details for certificates
  5. Balance invoiced automatically before the course

A paid deposit creates a private session and blocks the trainer's calendar, with no double entry.

Behind the scenes

The admin system is where most of the build lives. Everything the trainer needs to run the business sits in one place.

Two-way calendar sync

Sessions appear in her Google Calendar, and anything she marks busy stops new bookings clashing.

Enquiry and quote pipeline

Every workplace lead is tracked from first message to completed course, with quotes sent in a couple of clicks.

Emails she can edit

Confirmations, reminders, quotes and thank-yous run automatically, and she can change the wording herself.

Certificate-ready records

Accredited courses capture each learner's details, separate from whoever paid, and export them for registration.

Business dashboard

Revenue by course, bookings over time, deposits outstanding, class fill rates and enquiry conversion.

Design decisions

  • Pay in full for open classes, deposits only for workplace bookings. Small, fixed-price classes don't need the friction of a balance payment.
  • Separate the person paying from the people attending. One booking can cover a whole team, and each attendee gets their own record.
  • Guest checkout only. Nobody should need an account to book a first aid class.
  • Keep full accounting out of scope. The dashboard shows the numbers that matter, and bookkeeping stays with proper accounting software.
